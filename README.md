# Luciano Santana

AI Engineer at Konsi. I build, evaluate, and operate LLM agents and AI products in production. I also study Electrical Engineering at the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA) and am a member of LIAO, UFBA's AI and Optimization League.

My interests sit at the intersection of evals, AI safeguards, reinforcement learning, and optimization — especially evals and RL in high-risk sectors such as finance, law, and health, and scalable oversight.

## Work

At Konsi I am an **AI Engineer — LLM Agents, Evals & AI Products** (since December 2025). Recent work includes:

- A production crash-triage agent that investigates incidents from Crashlytics, BigQuery, and Sentry, then proposes fixes and opens pull requests. I migrated it to a GitHub App, integrated tools through MCP, and added Langfuse evaluations and tracing.
- Saldo Positivo, a finance content publisher: a Python pipeline on Cloud Run, using Gemini on Vertex AI, that surfaces emerging topics with embeddings and clustering over RSS. Deterministic content-safety gates check originality (n-grams and embedding similarity), require an official source for numeric or legal claims, block PII, and verify disclaimer and attribution; uncertain cases go to human review. A separate LLM-as-judge adversarial audit, with two Gemini judges, is used to pressure-test those gates.

Before this role, I spent three years on web and growth engineering at Konsi, mainly in Next.js and TypeScript.
